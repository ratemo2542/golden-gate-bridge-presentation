# The Golden Gate Bridge

An educational PowerPoint presentation exploring the Golden Gate Bridge's history, engineering, design, construction safety, and visitor experience.

## Presentation

Open **[Golden_Gate_Bridge.pptx](./Golden_Gate_Bridge.pptx)** to view the 8-slide presentation.

## Topics covered

1. Introduction to the Golden Gate Bridge
2. Key dimensions and engineering statistics
3. Planning, financing, construction, and opening timeline
4. The engineers and architect behind the bridge
5. Main-span comparison
6. Why the bridge is painted International Orange
7. Construction safety and the Halfway to Hell Club
8. Visiting the bridge and nearby viewpoints
